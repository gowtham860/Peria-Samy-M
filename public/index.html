Is SkillPath AI free to use?

Yes! You can generate roadmaps, use the AI tutor, complete practice quizzes, and track your progress at zero cost.

Does the AI Tutor really support Tamil?

Yes. The AI Tutor provides clear technical explanations and intuitive real-world analogies in both Tamil (தமிழ்) and English, keeping programming keywords clear and standard.

How are my learning plans and progress saved?

All roadmaps, task completion checks, and quiz attempts are securely stored under your isolated Firebase account using strict Cloud Firestore security rules.
